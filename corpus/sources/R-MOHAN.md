# Reviewer interpretations (Mohan Nadarajah, clinical reviewer)

These are expert rulings on how to apply payer text where the text is silent or its supporting tables are incomplete.
They are **not** payer policy. Each ruling is dated and quoted verbatim by an `interpretation` rule in `criteria.json`.

## R-2026-11-02-TNF (golden-set case GS-09)
Ruling (Mohan Nadarajah, 2 Nov 2026): For Texas Medicaid steps that ask about therapy with a TNF blocker, a drug that is a TNF blocker by its FDA label counts as TNF-blocker therapy even when the Texas TNF Blocker table does not list it, for example an infliximab infusion billed on the medical benefit or a newer adalimumab biosimilar such as adalimumab-aaty.

Context: the Texas TNF Blocker supporting table (S43, revised 10 Apr 2026) lists pharmacy products only; infliximab and adalimumab-aaty
(Yuflyma) do not appear anywhere in the document. Recorded in `golden_set/grades.json` (GS-09 note) and approved for the rule on 3 Nov 2026.
